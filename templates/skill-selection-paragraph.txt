When drafting the plan, analyse the context and pick the best active skill from `.workspace/skills/`. Both families are mandatory candidates on every selection — the upstream engineering catalogue (`mattpocock/skills`, generic engineering) and the product's own skills (`{{PRODUCT_SKILL_PREFIX}}-*`, product-specific rules); consult both before choosing. Pick the one most specific to the context; on a real coverage tie, `{{PRODUCT_SKILL_PREFIX}}-*` wins. Apply the skill while building the plan — not as a label added afterwards. Declare at the end: `Suggested skill: <name> · reason: <one line>` — or `none · <reason>` if nothing fits. Rule §8 of AGENTS.md.
